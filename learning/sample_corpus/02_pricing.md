# Nimbus DB — Pricing

Nimbus DB uses three pricing tiers, billed per cluster per hour:

- **Spark** — $0.12/hour. Up to 1 collection and 50,000 documents. No SLA.
- **Stratus** — $0.90/hour. Up to 20 collections and 5 million documents.
  99.9% uptime SLA.
- **Cumulus** — $4.50/hour. Unlimited collections, up to 200 million
  documents, 99.99% uptime SLA, and priority support.

Storage beyond the tier limit is billed at $0.20 per additional 100,000
documents per month. Similarity queries are free on all tiers; there is no
per-query charge. Data transfer *out* of a region costs $0.05 per GB; transfer
within the same region is free.

Annual prepay gives a 15% discount on the hourly rate. There is no free tier,
but new accounts receive a $25 credit that expires after 30 days.
